# Omkar Enterprise: Road Safety Hub

Create a professional, modern business website for "Omkar Enterprise" — a trusted manufacturer and supplier of Road Safety Products and Infrastructure/Landscaping solutions based in Maharashtra, India. The company works with both government contracts (like VVCMC tenders) and private clients, so the design should feel credible and government-trustworthy, yet modern and visually appealing — not boring or purely bureaucratic.

- Name: Omkar Enterprise
- Tagline: "The Road to Safety Starts With U"
- Established: Started as family business in 2015, incorporated in 2019
- Address: Shop No. 7, Vora Plaza, Navghar, Vasai (East), Dist. Palghar - 401203, Maharashtra, India
- Phone: +91 9673 66 4411
- Email: omkarenterprises@gmail.com
- GST No.: 27AKAPG1826C1ZS
- Manufacturing unit: Fully automatic plant with 20 TPD (Tons Per Day) capacity at Kursi, Barabanki (UP)
- Brand name of products: "Top Marc"
- Quality certification: Tested and certified from National Test Laboratory, Delhi
- Clients served: National Highway projects, PWD State Highways, Residential Societies, City Roads, Parking Zones, Airport Runways, and Municipal Corporations (like VVCMC)

WEBSITE GOAL:
Build a lead-generation + portfolio website — visitors should learn about the company, see completed work, and easily submit an inquiry (for both government tender-related work and private contracts).

PAGES / SECTIONS NEEDED:
1. Hero Section — company logo, tagline "The Road to Safety Starts With U", background image of a road/highway with crash barriers, CTA button "Get a Quote" / "Contact Us"
2. About Us — company history (2015 family business → 2019 incorporated), manufacturing capability, quality standards, mission statement
3. Our Services / Products (with icons and short descriptions for each):
   - Road Side Crash Barriers (single faced, double faced, single/double barrier variants)
   - RPM Cat's Eye (Raised Pavement Markers)
   - Median Marker (road studs)
   - Solar RPM (solar-powered LED road markers)
   - Delineator Rigid Retro Reflective Delineator
   - Thermoplastic Road Marking (Thermoplast)
   - FRP Outdoor Playground Equipment
   - Landscaping & Gardening (roadside plantation, median beautification)
4. Project Gallery — photo grid/carousel section showcasing completed government tender work and private projects (placeholder images for now, to be replaced with real project photos later). IMAGES / VISUALS:

For each service section, use relevant high-quality stock/placeholder images that visually represent the actual product — for example:

- Road Side Crash Barriers: images of highway guardrails/crash barriers on curved roads

- RPM Cat's Eye & Median Marker: images of yellow/red reflective road studs

- Solar RPM: image of solar-powered road stud with LED

- Delineator: image of reflective roadside delineator posts

- Thermoplast: image of yellow thermoplastic road marking/zebra crossing

- FRP Outdoor Playground Equipment: image of colorful playground slides/swings setup

- Landscaping & Gardening: image of roadside median with plants, flowers, and palm trees

Use good quality, realistic stock images so the site looks visually complete and professional from the start (not empty placeholders). These will later be replaced with the client's own real project photos, so keep the image containers/sections easy to swap out afterward.
5. Why Choose Us — quality certifications, tender/government project experience, timely delivery, ISO standard products
6. Inquiry / Contact Form — Fields: Name, Phone, Email, Company/Organization (optional), Project Type (dropdown: Government Tender / Private Contract / Product Inquiry), Message. Form should send to omkarenterprises@gmail.com
7. Contact & Footer — Address, phone (clickable), email (clickable), GST number displayed, Google Maps embed for Vora Plaza, Navghar, Vasai (East), Palghar, social links (optional), copyright

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:
- Color palette: Blue and orange (matching the existing Omkar Enterprise logo colors), with white/light backgrounds — professional and trustworthy but not dull
- Style: Clean, modern, corporate — should feel credible for government tender clients AND appealing enough for private customers
- Fully responsive (mobile + desktop)
- Include icons for each service (road safety themed icons — barriers, road markers, solar panel, playground, plants)
- Smooth scroll navigation with sticky header (Logo | Home | About | Services | Gallery | Contact)
- Include a floating "Call Now" or WhatsApp button linked to +91 9673664411 for quick contact

LANGUAGE:
- Website should support THREE languages: English, Hindi, and Marathi
- Add a language switcher (dropdown or toggle) in the header — EN / हिं / मरा
- Default language: English

ADDITIONAL NOTES:
- This is a real business serving municipal corporations (like VVCMC) and highway/infrastructure clients, so trust signals (GST number, years of experience, certifications, client types) should be prominently visible
- Keep the tone professional but not overly government-formal — balance credibility with modern appeal since private clients are also a target audience
